# Marketing & Branding Plan - DataWrangler.AI

This document serves as the single source of truth for DataWrangler.AI's marketing strategy, target audience, brand tone, and value propositions. It should be referenced alongside the `IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` to ensure technical execution aligns with our product positioning.

---

## 🎯 1. Product Vision & Positioning

**Tagline:** "The Bloomberg Terminal for Soccer Analytics"

**Core Pivot:**
We are transitioning the platform's primary public positioning from a "Sports Betting Application" to a "Soccer Analytics Application." The intent is to broaden the appeal so that general soccer fans and data enthusiasts find it valuable, while still providing the deep, actionable insights that sports bettors rely on.

**Scope Expansion:**
Moving beyond just the English Premier League (EPL) to embrace the concept of **Global Soccer**.

---

## 👥 2. Target Audience & Tone

**Primary Audiences:**
1. **Sports Bettors (The Silent Core):** The platform should still deeply appeal to bettors by providing the data they need to find edges (e.g., ROI, odds, trends). However, we won't aggressively market to them with explicit "gambling" language. 
2. **Soccer Analytics Enthusiasts:** Fans, data nerds, and amateur analysts who want deep historical context and head-to-head metrics without needing to understand complex mathematical models.

**Brand Tone:**
- **Data-Driven but Accessible:** Do not be "too mathy." The insights should be easy to digest and visually intuitive.
- **Professional & Premium:** Like a Bloomberg terminal—authoritative, clean, and serious about data.
- **Subtle on Betting:** It's okay to include betting-adjacent metrics inside the app, but the main public pitch is "understanding the game better through data."

---

## 🚀 3. Key Value Propositions to Highlight

When marketing or building landing pages, emphasize these core features:
- **Historical Team Performance:** Easily track how teams have performed over time across various contexts.
- **Head-to-Head Matchday Trends:** Uncover historical patterns when specific teams face off.
- **Global Soccer Coverage (Vision vs. Beta Reality):** Our overarching vision is a unified view of soccer data from around the world. **General Rule of Thumb:** For the current Beta phase, coverage is strictly limited to English leagues (Premier League and Championship). All marketing materials should reflect that we are starting with deep English coverage and have aggressive plans to expand globally in the future.


---

## 🧪 4. Pricing & BETA Strategy

**Current Status:** BETA Testing
- The product is heavily under development. 
- We are currently deploying a beta version to collect feedback, understand usage patterns, and gather data.
- **Pro Access / Pricing:** The software is currently **not for sale**. Pricing tiers on the landing page should clearly reflect that this is a BETA phase (e.g., "$0/month for Beta Access"). Our current goal is user acquisition and product feedback, not immediate monetization.

---

## ❓ 5. Open Questions & Future Explorations

*These are ongoing questions to help us further align on the marketing and product intent as we continue development. We can answer these as we go.*

1. **User Onboarding:** Since we are in BETA, how do we want to handle the "Getting Started" flow? Should it be an open registration, or a waitlist/invite-only model to control the beta pool?
2. **Global Soccer Rollout:** Since we are moving to global soccer, which leagues are we targeting next after the EPL? (e.g., La Liga, Serie A, MLS, Champions League?)
3. **Betting Metrics Integration:** Which specific betting metrics (e.g., closing line value, over/under trends) are must-haves for our "silent core" of bettors, even if we don't put them on the main marketing landing page?
4. **Beta Feedback Loop:** How are we collecting feedback from these early beta users? Is there an in-app feedback form, a Discord community, or email surveys?
5. **Marketing Channels:** Where are we planning to find our initial beta testers? (e.g., Twitter/X, Reddit soccer communities, betting forums, SEO?)
